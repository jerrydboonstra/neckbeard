# Working rules

A sample reader for neckbeard's example reports: the rules a fictional developer
already runs. Every report in `examples/` compares its target against these files
and nothing else. Two of them arrive through imports, as real setups often do.

@rules/safety.md
@rules/engineering.md

## How I like answers

- Give the reasoning with the verdict. A review that says "looks fine" without
  saying what was checked is not a review.
- Plain language over jargon. Short is good; complete is required.
